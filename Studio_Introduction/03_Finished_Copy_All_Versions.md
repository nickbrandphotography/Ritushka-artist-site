## Page 3 — Why designers commission rather than source

**Headline**
> Three reasons a client pays for an original.

**01 — Scale**
> Above about 1,600 mm the market thins out fast, and what remains is usually a print. A painting made to the wall is often the only way to hold a double-height space without filling it with three smaller works that fight each other.

**02 — Control of the palette**
> A sourced work is a compromise between what exists and what the room needs. A commissioned work starts from your scheme. If the stone shifts warmer at specification stage, the painting shifts with it.

**03 — It cannot be found anywhere else**
> Clients who have spent two years on a house notice when the artwork is available online. An original with a signed certificate, a title and a documented provenance reads differently — and it is the one element of the scheme that appreciates rather than dates.
